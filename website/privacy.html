AirBridge Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026 · Applies to the AirBridge Android app, the AirBridge desktop service (macOS, Windows, Linux) and the web interface it serves. The public copy of this policy is https://airbridge.fyi/privacy.html.

The short version

AirBridge moves your data between your own devices — the Android phone and the computer you paired with it. It has no accounts, no cloud, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Data travels over your local network (or the phone's own hotspot), end-to-end encrypted between the two paired devices. The AirBridge project never receives it.

Who is responsible

AirBridge is an open-source project by Diganta Dey and contributors. Contact: hello@airbridge.fyi.

AirBridge never: reads what you type or passwords; reads the rest of the screen through Accessibility; opens the microphone; records or logs calls; reads your call log or SMS; sells, shares or uses data for advertising.

Accessibility service (optional)

The optional "AirBridge clipboard bridge" Accessibility service exists only so text you copy in other apps can reach your paired computer while AirBridge is in the background. It is off by default, is enabled only after you agree to an in-app disclosure and switch it on in Android Settings, does not retrieve window content, does not perform actions, and can be turned off at any time.

Screen sharing and audio

Every session needs your approval in Android's own "start recording or casting" prompt, shows a persistent notification, and can be stopped from the notification or the app. Sound uses Android's playback-capture API, which captures only media/game audio that the playing app allows; it cannot capture the microphone or calls. Apps that protect their content (for example Netflix, Prime Video, Hotstar) appear black — AirBridge does not bypass this.

Storage and retention

  • Transfer history, clipboard history (last 50 items) and settings stay on the device that created them until you clear them or uninstall.
  • Pairing keys are stored on-device and are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
  • Contacts, call details, notifications and screen frames are not stored by the computer; they are shown while connected and discarded.
  • Received files are saved where you choose (default ~/Downloads/AirBridge on a computer, Downloads on Android).

Security

Sessions use per-pair keys, AES-GCM encryption and a six-digit verification code you can compare on both screens. See SECURITY.md for the threat model and how to report vulnerabilities.

Network connections the project can see

None by default. The desktop service may contact api.github.com to check for updates, and crash reports are only sent if you review and submit them (as a GitHub issue or to an endpoint you configure). The Android app makes no connections other than to your paired computer.

Children

AirBridge is a general-purpose utility not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Your choices

Every feature above has its own switch in Settings, every Android permission can be revoked in Android Settings, and uninstalling removes all app data. Because the project holds no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete — but you can contact us with any question.

Changes

Changes are published in this file (and on the website) with a new effective date. Material changes are called out in the release notes.
