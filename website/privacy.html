PRIVACY AIRBRIDGE

Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

AirBridge is built to move information directly between devices you choose. This policy explains the information handled by the Android app, desktop companion and AirBridge website.

Summary

AirBridge does not require an account and does not sell personal data. The Android app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, Accessibility Service or notification-listener service. It does not upload your selected content to an AirBridge cloud service.

Information handled to provide the feature you choose

  • Files and media. Files are read only after you select or share them, then sent to the paired device you choose. Received files are stored on the receiving device.
  • Clipboard content. Text or images are sent when you use an explicit AirBridge action, or when Android permits the optional automatic clipboard setting. Android may block hidden clipboard reads; AirBridge does not use Accessibility access to bypass that rule.
  • Contacts and phone controls. These optional features are disabled until you turn them on and grant Android’s matching permission. Contact data is sent only to a paired computer while the feature is on. AirBridge does not request call-log access.
  • Screen sharing. A paired computer can request a visual screen share only after you approve it in AirBridge and in Android’s system capture prompt. Application audio is not captured. DRM-protected video may be blank by design.
  • Connection data. The app stores the paired computer address, device identity material, encrypted-pairing state and TLS trust pin locally so it can reconnect safely. Android backup is disabled for this app.

How information moves

AirBridge encrypts supported transfer and continuity content between paired devices. On a local network it connects directly where possible. If you deliberately configure or accept a relay route, the relay carries encrypted traffic; it is not given the content encryption keys. As with any network connection, the participating devices and network operator can observe ordinary connection metadata such as addresses and timing.

Permissions

Camera access is requested only when you open the QR scanner. Notifications support visible transfer progress and incoming offers. Contacts, phone controls and media projection are requested only when you enable the corresponding optional feature. Nearby Wi-Fi and Bluetooth capabilities support nearby discovery and optional hotspot handoff. You can revoke Android permissions at any time in system settings.

Sharing and retention

AirBridge shares selected content only with the paired peer or peers involved in the action. It does not sell or use it for advertising. Transfer history and clipboard history are kept on the participating devices subject to the product’s limits; you can clear clipboard history, delete received files, revoke permissions or use Forget to remove the saved pairing from Android.

Website

The public website is a static site. It does not require a sign-in or set advertising cookies. Links to GitHub and email are governed by those services when you choose to follow or use them.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as AirBridge changes. Material updates will carry a new effective date on this page. For privacy questions or deletion help, contact hello@airbridge.fyi.
